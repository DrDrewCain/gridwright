# Data attribution

The `.m` files in this directory are IEEE test cases as distributed by the
**IEEE PES Power Grid Library — Optimal Power Flow** (PGLib-OPF), v23.07.

- Source: <https://github.com/power-grid-lib/pglib-opf>
- Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** (CC BY 4.0),
  <http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
- The underlying IEEE cases originate from the University of Washington power
  systems test case archive, Copyright (c) 1999 Richard D. Christie,
  University of Washington, also under CC BY 4.0.

They are included unmodified, so that the tests in
`crates/gridwright-solve/tests/real_networks.rs` validate against networks
nobody here designed. A synthetic topology cannot tell you the physics is
wrong, because it was generated by the same assumptions being tested.

Note that gridwright solves these as **DC** optimal power flow. Reactive power,
voltage magnitudes and shunt admittances are ignored, and generator costs are
taken from the linear term of the published quadratic. The objective values are
therefore not comparable to published AC-OPF results, and no such comparison is
claimed anywhere in this repository.
